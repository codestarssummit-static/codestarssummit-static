Friday 3/28 9to5 (All Day)

Fun with Functions in JavaScript (Full Day)

Douglas Crockford
Workshop leader

Workshop detail

This one day hands-on workshop is about using functions and thinking with functions to take your JavaScript use to another level. This workshop will challenge you to do better and think differently. Are you game?

WHAT WILL YOU LEARN

Through a graduated series of problems, your knowledge of what is possible with Javascript will grow exponentially.

WHO SHOULD ATTEND

Developers, Operations Engineers and Testers and anyone wanting to increase their skill in functions and JavaScript.



This is a BYOL (Bring Your Own Laptop) workshop

Douglas Crockford

crockford.com
